# Bathroom renovation

Renovate the existing bathroom. The room size is not changing.

Two teams: the **tiler** does the tiling (phase 5); the **builder** does everything else.

## Size

| | mm |
|---|---|
| Length (window wall to door wall) | **2,500** |
| Width (along the window wall) | **1,380** |
| Ceiling height | **2,800** |

Floor and ceiling area: 2.5 × 1.38 = **3.45 m²**.

| # | Phase | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|

### 1. Preparation

Before anything else:
1. **Take the old door and its hinges off** first, so there is room to work. The frame stays; the new door goes in last (phase 6).
2. Clean **all the pink/purple plastic tile edge trims in the bathroom** properly — they have old paint and dirt on them.
3. **Tape out the existing wall tiles** so no paint gets on them: along the walls (on the pink edge trim) and down the door frame.
4. Wrap the shower, water heater, soap holder and toilet paper holder in plastic, and **tape the shower and the water heater out** at the wall — no paint on them.
5. **Take the window's steel security screen off** and keep it safe — it is painted off the wall in phase 4, then goes back on.

The plastic and tape stay on for the whole job and come off only when the job is done, so everything is protected through the strip out, ceiling, painting, tiling and fit-out.

### 2. Strip out

Remove **everything** in the bathroom — including the old ceiling: take down the ceiling light (**leave its cable in place** — the new light goes on that cable later), then the whole old ceiling, tiles and grid — **except these, which stay**:

| Stays | |
|---|---|
| Floor tiles | the new tiles go on top of them — don't lift them |
| Shower | the existing shower |
| Water heater | the existing shower water heater |
| Soap holder | the existing one |
| Toilet paper holder | the existing one |


### 3. Ceiling

A new flush gypsum ceiling.

**Steps**
1. **Frame:** galvanised steel wall angle round the room at the old ceiling height (2,800 mm); C-channels across the room (the 1,380 mm span) at 40 cm centres, resting on the wall angle on both long walls — the span is short, so no hanger wire.
2. **Board:** screw up moisture-resistant gypsum board over the whole ceiling, for a flush finish — bring the ceiling light's cable out through the board where the light goes.
3. **Joints:** tape and fill every joint and screw head smooth — **including the corner where the ceiling meets the walls, all round the room (7.76 m)**: fold the tape into the corner and fill it, so it doesn't crack.

### 4. Painting

The trims, shower and water heater are already taped out (phase 1). With the white paint already on site:

1. **Clean** the ceiling, the walls, the window frame and the security screen.
2. **Paint:**
   - the ceiling and the walls;
   - the window frame;
   - the steel security screen, **off the wall** (it came off in phase 1), painted on its own.
3. **Screen back on:** when the paint on the screen and the window frame is dry, fix the screen back on the window — before the tiling.

### 5. Tiling

**Done by the tiler** — a separate team from the builder.

The new grey tiles go **on top of the old floor tiles** — the old ones stay. The floor, the new drain grate, and the riser (the upright face) of the floor step, tiled up to meet the **existing pink plastic edge trim on top of the step**, which stays.

- Floor 2,500 × 1,380 mm: 5 tiles along × 3 rows (the third row cut to 380 mm) = **15 tiles**.

# Bathroom renovation

Renovate the existing bathroom. The room size is not changing.

Two teams: the **tiler** does the tiling (phase 5); the **builder** does everything else.

## Size

| | mm |
|---|---|
| Length (window wall to door wall) | **2,500** |
| Width (along the window wall) | **1,380** |
| Ceiling height | **2,800** |

Floor and ceiling area: 2.5 × 1.38 = **3.45 m²**.

## The work, in order

| # | Phase | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preparation | Builder | Take off the door and screen, clean the trims, protect what stays |
| 2 | Strip out | Builder | Remove the rest |
| 3 | Ceiling | Builder | New ceiling |
| 4 | Painting | Builder | Ceiling, walls, window frame and security screen |
| 5 | Tiling | **Tiler** | Floor and step |
| 6 | Fit-out | Builder | Fittings, then the new door |

### 1. Preparation

Before anything else.

**Take off:**
1. **The old door and its hinges** — first, so there is room to work. The frame stays; the new door goes in last (phase 6).
2. **The window's steel security screen** — keep it safe; it is painted in phase 4 and goes back on.

**Clean:** all the pink/purple plastic tile edge trims in the bathroom — they have old paint and dirt on them.

**Protect what stays.** The tape and plastic stay on for the whole job and come off only when the job is done:

| Stays | Protect it |
|---|---|
| Wall tiles | tape them out: along the walls (on the pink edge trim) and down the door frame |
| Shower | wrap in plastic; tape it out at the wall |
| Water heater | wrap in plastic; tape it out at the wall |
| Soap holder | wrap in plastic |
| Toilet paper holder | wrap in plastic |
| Floor tiles | don't lift them — the new tiles go on top |
| Toilet stop valve and hose | keep — they go on the new toilet |
| Bidet spray and its hose | wrap in plastic |

### 2. Strip out

Remove **everything else**, the old ceiling included: take down the ceiling light (**leave its cable in place** — the new light goes on that cable later), then the whole old ceiling, tiles and grid. Everything in the "Stays" table (phase 1) stays.

Take the old basin's wall brackets off with the basin — they have rusted. The new basin covers the old holes.

### 3. Ceiling

A new flush gypsum ceiling.

**Steps**
1. **Frame:** galvanised steel wall angle round the room at the old ceiling height (2,800 mm); C-channels across the room (the 1,380 mm span) at 40 cm centres, resting on the wall angle on both long walls.
2. **Board:** screw up moisture-resistant gypsum board over the whole ceiling, for a flush finish — bring the ceiling light's cable out through the board where the light goes.
3. **Joints:** tape and fill every joint and screw head smooth — **including the corner where the ceiling meets the walls, all round the room (7.76 m)**: fold the tape into the corner and fill it, so it doesn't crack.

### 4. Painting

Everything that stays is already protected (phase 1). With the white paint already on site:

1. **Clean** the ceiling, the walls, the window frame and the security screen.
2. **Paint:**
   - the ceiling and the walls;
   - the window frame;
   - the steel security screen, **off the wall** (it came off in phase 1), painted on its own.
3. **Screen back on:** when the paint on the screen and the window frame is dry, fix the screen back on the window — before the tiling.

### 5. Tiling

**Done by the tiler.** Tile the floor **on top of the old floor tiles**, set the new drain grate, and tile the step's riser (its upright face) up to the existing pink plastic edge trim on top of the step.

**Tiles** (500 × 500 mm, 4 to a box):
- Floor 2,500 × 1,380 mm: 5 tiles along × 3 rows, the third row cut to 380 mm = **15 tiles**.
- Step riser 1,380 × 50 mm: cut from the third row's offcuts (120 × 500 mm each), so no extra tile.
- With the box to buy there are 16 tiles: **1 spare**.

**How:**
- Cement-based tile adhesive, not sand-cement mortar.
- Joints **1.5 mm, all the same** — the tiler's own levelling clips and wedges keep the spacing and the tile edges flush.
- Grout every joint, **including where the new floor meets the old wall tiles**. **No silicone.**
- The new box must be **shade H07**, the same as the 3 on site — check the box label.

## Installation (electrician)

Each light goes on an existing switched ceiling point.

- **KÄRRNOCKA:** screw the steel base to the ceiling tile with the screws in the box, wire the ceiling cable into the terminal block on the base, twist the shade on. Instructions: <https://www.ikea.com/th/en/assembly_instructions/kaerrnocka-ceiling-lamp-white__AA-2595673-2-100.pdf>
- **Track light (living room):** screw the track to the ceiling, wire it like the existing one.
- **L&E bulkhead:** screw flat to the underside of the eaves, wire inside the fitting.

# Lighting

Ceiling lights for the 2 bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom and living room, and 3 lights under the outside eaves: what goes where, what was ordered, and how it is fitted.

> **IMPORTANT — every lamp screws straight on. No hooks, no hanging lamps.**
> - **KÄRRNOCKA** (IKEA) screws into the ceiling tile with the screws in its box. IKEA's web page says "to be hung on a ceiling hook" — ignore that.
> - **L&E 36-00152 bulkhead** (HomePro) screws to the underside of the eaves. The eaves are not a suspended ceiling.

Everything is in [2026-11-05-01-lighting.md](2026-11-05-01-lighting.md): the lamp and bulbs in each room, the orders (IKEA, Shopee, HomePro) with their order numbers and how to find their emails, the products, and the installation.
